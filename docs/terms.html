Purpose

Herkos is a research prototype

The site presents a hackathon project that measures exit capacity for FXRP lending. You may view the site and use the documentation for evaluation, research, and development.

The public result includes live reads and a pinned fork snapshot. It does not represent a promise that any lending market has adopted the oracle.

Coston2 testnet

Test assets have no monetary value

The Coston2 page is an experimental, unaudited testnet market. Faucet FTestXRP and USDT0 are provided for testing only. They have no monetary value, yield, or claim on a Mainnet asset.

Testnet contracts may be reset, replaced, paused, or stop working. A transaction shown as confirmed proves only what happened on Coston2 at that time.
